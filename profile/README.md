# Mazo Maps 🌍

### Mapping the world, together.

**Mazo Maps** is an open-source mapping platform for accurate, detailed, and accessible maps worldwide. Explore, contribute, and improve geographic data together.

## Our mission

Make geographic information open, useful, and accessible to people everywhere, while respecting privacy, source licensing, and local knowledge.

## What we're building

- **Explore:** Discover geographic information around the world.
- **Contribute:** Suggest corrections and additions to geographic data.
- **Build:** Create open-source mapping tools and services.
- **Collaborate:** Review changes transparently and improve data quality.

## Get involved

Browse our [repositories](https://github.com/orgs/mazo-maps/repositories), open an issue, or read our [contribution guide](https://github.com/mazo-maps/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

**Open maps. Open data. Open possibilities.**
