# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Mazo Maps to be a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for contributors around the world, regardless of background, identity, experience, or location.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, including during disagreements.
- Welcome newcomers and different perspectives.
- Give credit for contributions and geographic knowledge.
- Respect privacy, safety, and local communities.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Deliberate vandalism or malicious geographic edits.
- Repeated disruptive behavior after moderation requests.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to organization maintainers through an appropriate private contact channel listed in the relevant repository. Do not publish sensitive reports in public issues. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban accounts when necessary. Reports will be handled with discretion; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed in every circumstance.

## Scope

This code applies to Mazo Maps project spaces and when representing the project publicly.

This is a project-specific policy inspired by common open-source community standards.
